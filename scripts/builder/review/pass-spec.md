Does the change do what the spec and the approved design require: all of it, in the places the design names, in the shape the design describes, and nothing it forbids? Walk every requirement's acceptance criteria and every contract and its readers. Look for paths the design names that were not changed, logic placed somewhere other than where the design says (for example duplicated instead of in the one place the design asks for), controls or options the spec does not allow, and changes to files or behaviour the design does not cover.
