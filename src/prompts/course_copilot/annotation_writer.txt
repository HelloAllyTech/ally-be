THE TRANSCRIPT ("units")

- Speakers by role: the helper as this audience is called ("Listener", "Counsellor", "Peer supporter") and the person ("Caller", "Client", "Texter"). Give the person a fictional first name in the intro, not in the speaker field.
- A realistic conversation in this audience's real setting (brief.audience), with a beginning, a turning point and an ending. Real speech: short turns, hesitations, a person who sometimes deflects or tests the helper.
