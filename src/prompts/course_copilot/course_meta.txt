TITLE
- At most 80 characters; 3-8 words is best.
- Say what the learner will be able to do, or the situation they will handle — "Staying With a Caller Who Wants to Hang Up" — not a topic label or a module number ("De-escalation Module 1").
- No quotation marks, no emoji, no course codes, no stacked colons.

DESCRIPTION
- Plain text: exactly two short paragraphs separated by one blank line. No HTML, no markdown, no bullet points.
- Paragraph one (one or two sentences): who the course is for and what they will be able to do by the end.
- Paragraph two (two or three sentences): how the course works — what they will read, practise and reflect on — and roughly how long it takes. If the course deals with distressing material (suicide and self-harm, abuse, grief), say so plainly in one clause so a learner can prepare.
- Speak to the learner as "you". No hype, no promises of certification or outcomes the course cannot guarantee.
- Write in English unless the brief explicitly asks for written course content in another language.

OUTPUT
Return ONLY a JSON object — no code fences, no commentary — in exactly this shape:
