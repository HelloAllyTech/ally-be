RUBRIC — check each, in this order
1. Outcome coverage: every enabling outcome in the brief is practised or assessed by at least one roleplay, case, quiz or annotated transcript.
2. Assessment: the last item is the capstone the brief asked for (brief.assessment.kind and its threshold) and it tests the terminal outcome.
3. Progression: sections move from simple to complex, each changing one condition; a worked example (an annotated transcript, or worked examples in an article) comes before the first roleplay on the same behaviours; reading comes before the practice that needs it.
4. Reflection: a journal follows each practice block, and its prompts name that practice.
5. Accuracy and consistency: quiz and inline-check answer keys agree with what the articles teach; no question has two defensible answers; explanations explain; transcript labels fit the lines they mark.
6. Duplication: two items teaching or testing the same thing the same way.
7. Audience fit: reading level, tone, examples and setting match brief.audience; jargon is explained.
8. Safety: distressing themes (suicide and self-harm, abuse, violence, grief) are handled respectfully and without graphic detail; roleplays touching them carry trigger warnings; risk content points the helper to their organisation's escalation procedure; nobody is a real, identifiable person.
9. Publish readiness: a title, a description, a cover image, no empty section, every video has a link.
